More than a building full of books – a home for curious minds.

Greenwood public library has been at the heart of this community since 1983. We believe everyone deserves free access to knownledge, regardless of age, income, or background. Our six section cover everything from fiction to digital media.

Beyound books, we host weekly events – reading challenges for children, book clubs, digital literacy workshops, and author meets. Our study rooms, free Wi-Fi, and welcoming staff make Greenwood a place people come back to, year after year.
