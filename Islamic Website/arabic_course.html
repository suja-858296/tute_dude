Course Syllabus

Module 1: Introduction to Arabic Grammar (Nahw)

Understanding word categories: Ism (Noun), Fi'l (Verb), and Harf (Particle), along with basic sentence structures.

Module 2: Arabic Morphology (Sarf - Part 1)

Learning 3-letter root word patterns, verb conjugations in the past tense (Maadi) and present/future tense (Mudaari).

Module 3: Nominal Sentences (Al-Jumlah Al-Ismiyyah)

Analyzing the subject (Mubtada) and predicate (Khabar), and their grammatical states (Marfoo, Mansoob, Majroor).

Module 4: Verbal Sentences (Al-Jumlah Al-Fi'liyyah)

Mastering the structure of verb, subject (Faa'il), and direct object (Maf'ool Bihi).

Module 5: Quranic Vocabulary & Translation Exercises

Applying grammar rules directly to high-frequency Quranic verses to build independent reading skills.
