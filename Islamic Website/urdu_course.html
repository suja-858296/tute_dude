Course Syllabus

Module 1: Advanced Grammar & Idiomatic Expressions

In-depth study of complex Urdu syntax, compound words (Tarkeebat), and traditional proverbs (Muhavare).

Module 2: Classical Prose Analysis (Nasr)

Reading and analyzing works from prominent literary figures such as Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and Mirza Ghalib.

Module 3: Introduction to Urdu Poetry (Shayari)

Understanding poetic structures including Ghazal, Nazm, and Rubai, along with meter and rhyme schemes.

Module 4: Academic Writing & Composition

Developing techniques for essay writing, literary critique, and formal translation from English to Urdu.

Module 5: Contemporary Urdu Literature

Exploring modern 20th and 21st-century Urdu short stories (Afsane) and contemporary discourse.
