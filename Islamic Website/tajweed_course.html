Course Syllabus

Module 1: Makharij (Points of Articulation)

Understanding exactly where each Arabic letter originates from the throat, tongue, and lips.

Module 2: Sifaat (Characteristics of Letters)

Learning the permanent and temporary qualities of letters, including heavy and light letters.

Module 3: Rules of Noon Sakinah & Tanween

Mastering Izhaar, Idghaam, Iqlab, and Ikhfaa.

Module 4: Rules of Meem Sakinah

Understanding Ikhfaa Shafawi, Idghaam Shafawi, and Izhaar Shafawi.

Module 5: Al-Mudood (Elongations)

The rules of stretching vowels, including Madd Asli and Madd Far'i.
